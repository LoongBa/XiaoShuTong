# ===== 九年级全一册 =====

## Unit 1 How can we become good learners?
- **核心句型**：How can I...? / You should...
- **易混点**：How can I（我怎样才能...）vs What should I（我应该...）
- **口诀**："How can I怎样才能good learners好学习者study学习group小组"

## Unit 2 I think that mooncakes are delicious!
- **核心句型**：I think that... / I wonder if/whether...
- **易混点**：I think that（我认为...）vs I wonder if（我想知道是否...）
- **口诀**："I think that我认为I wonder if我想知道是否mooncakes月饼delicious美味的"

## Unit 3 Could you please tell me where the restrooms are?
- **核心句型**：Could you tell me where... are?
- **易混点**：宾语从句用陈述语序（where... are）vs 疑问句用倒装语序（where are...）
- **口诀**："Could you tell me你能告诉我吗where restrooms洗手间central中心的"

## Unit 4 I used to be afraid of the dark.
- **核心句型**：I used to... / I didn't use to...
- **易混点**：used to（过去常常）vs be used to（习惯于）
- **口诀**："I used to我过去常常afraid害怕dark黑暗silent沉默public公共的private私人的"

## Unit 5 What are the shirts made of?
- **核心句型**：What is/are... made of/from/in?
- **易混点**：made of（看得出原材料）vs made from（看不出原材料）vs made in（在...制造）
- **口诀**："What are... made of由...制成of看得出from看不出in在...制造produce生产process加工product产品"

## Unit 6 When was it invented?
- **核心句型**：When was it invented? / Who was it invented by?
- **易混点**：was invented（被发明，被动语态）vs invented（发明了，主动语态）
- **口诀**："When was it invented它什么时候被发明的invent发明telephone电话wheel轮子electricity电camera相机"

## Unit 7 Teenagers should be allowed to choose their own clothes.
- **核心句型**：Teenagers should be allowed to...
- **易混点**：should be allowed to（应该被允许做）vs can be allowed to（可以被允许做）
- **口诀**："Teenagers青少年should be allowed to应该被允许drive驾驶safety安全earring耳环concentrate集中"

## Unit 8 It must belong to Carla.
- **核心句型**：It must/might/can't be...
- **易混点**：must（一定，肯定推测）vs might/could（可能，不确定推测）vs can't（不可能，否定推测）
- **口诀**："It must be一定是It might be可能是It can't be不可能belong to属于"

## Unit 9 I like music that I can dance to.
- **核心句型**：I like... that/who...
- **易混点**：that（关系代词，指物）vs who（关系代词，指人）
- **口诀**："I like music that I can dance to定语从句that指物who指人lyrics歌词Australian澳大利亚的"

## Unit 10 You're supposed to shake hands.
- **核心句型**：You're supposed to...
- **易混点**：be supposed to（应该做，按惯例）vs should（应该做，一般建议）
- **口诀**："You're supposed to你应该bow鞠躬kiss亲吻greet问候value重视drop by拜访"

## Unit 11 Sad movies make me cry.
- **核心句型**：...make(s) sb. (feel)...
- **易混点**：make sb. do（使某人做某事）vs make sb. + adj.（使某人怎么样）
- **口诀**："make使役动词make sb. do使某人做make sb. adj.使某人怎么样leave离开pull拉"

## Unit 12 Life is full of the unexpected.
- **核心句型**：By the time..., I had...
- **易混点**：By the time（到...时候，过去完成时标志）vs When（当...时候，一般过去时）
- **口诀**："Life is full of充满意外By the time到...时候unexpected意想不到的go off响起wake up醒来"

## Unit 13 We're trying to save the earth!
- **核心句型**：We're trying to...
- **易混点**：We're trying to（我们正在努力...）vs We try to（我们努力...）
- **口诀**："We're trying to我们正在努力save拯救earth地球litter乱扔垃圾advantage优势cost花费recycle回收reuse重复利用"

## Unit 14 I remember meeting all of you in Grade 7.
- **核心句型**：I remember... / I'm looking forward to...
- **易混点**：remember doing（记得做过）vs remember to do（记得要做）
- **口诀**："I remember meeting我记得见过I'm looking forward to我期待着meet the standard达到标准"
