# ===== 八年级上册 =====

## Unit 1 Where did you go on vacation?
- **核心句型**：Where did you go on vacation? / I went to...
- **易混点**：went（go的过去式）vs gone（go的过去分词）
- **口诀**："Where did you go你去了哪里beach海滩museum博物馆vacation假期"

## Unit 2 How often do you exercise?
- **核心句型**：How often do you...?
- **易混点**：How often（多久一次）vs How many（多少）vs How much（多少钱/多少）
- **口诀**："How often问频率always总是usually通常often经常sometimes有时hardly ever几乎不"

## Unit 3 I'm more outgoing than my sister.
- **核心句型**：...more... than... / ...-er than...
- **易混点**：more+多音节词（比较级）vs -er+单音节词（比较级）
- **口诀**："比较级more或-er外向outgoing有趣funny安静quiet"

## Unit 4 What's the best movie theater?
- **核心句型**：What's the best...? / It's the most...
- **易混点**：best（最好的，good/well的最高级）vs most（最多的，many/much的最高级）
- **口诀**："最高级best或most电影院theater舒适的comfortable座位seat"

## Unit 5 Do you want to watch a game show?
- **核心句型**：Do you want to watch...?
- **易混点**：Do you want to watch（你想看吗？）vs Do you like（你喜欢吗？）
- **口诀**："Do you want to watch你想看吗game show游戏节目talk show脱口秀news新闻"

## Unit 6 I'm going to study computer science.
- **核心句型**：What are you going to be?
- **易混点**：be going to（将来时，打算）vs will（将来时，预测/意愿）
- **口诀**："be going to打算做computer science计算机科学engineer工程师cook厨师"

## Unit 7 Will people have robots?
- **核心句型**：Will there be...? / Will you...?
- **易混点**：Will there be（将来会有吗？）vs Will you（你会吗？）
- **口诀**："Will将来时robots机器人fewer更少（可数）less更少（不可数）more更多"

## Unit 8 How do you make a banana milkshake?
- **核心句型**：How do you make a...?
- **易混点**：turn on（打开）vs turn off（关闭）、cut up（切碎）vs cut down（砍倒）
- **口诀**："How do you make你怎么制作turn on打开blender搅拌机cut up切碎pour倒入"

## Unit 9 Can you come to my party?
- **核心句型**：Can you come to...? / Sorry, I can't...
- **易混点**：Can you come（你能来吗？）vs Could you come（你能来吗？，更礼貌）
- **口诀**："Can you come你能来吗party聚会prepare准备dentist牙医"

## Unit 10 If you go to the party, you'll have a great time!
- **核心句型**：If you..., you'll...
- **易混点**：If（如果，条件状语从句）vs When（当...时候，时间状语从句）
- **口诀**："If条件句将来时will用在主句不在从句organize组织have a great time玩得开心"

# ===== 八年级下册 =====

## Unit 1 What's the matter?
- **核心句型**：What's the matter? / I have a...
- **易混点**：What's the matter?（怎么了？）vs What's wrong?（怎么了？）
- **口诀**："What's the matter怎么了cold感冒stomachache胃疼headache头疼toothache牙疼"

## Unit 2 I'll help to clean up the city parks.
- **核心句型**：I'll... / You could...
- **易混点**：I'll（我将要）vs You could（你可以，建议）
- **口诀**："I'll我将要help帮助clean up打扫city parks城市公园give out分发volunteer志愿者"

## Unit 3 Could you please clean your room?
- **核心句型**：Could you please...?
- **易混点**：Could you please（你能...吗？，更礼貌）vs Can you（你能...吗？）
- **口诀**："Could you please你能...吗clean打扫room房间dishes碗rubbish垃圾clothes衣服"

## Unit 4 Why don't you talk to your parents?
- **核心句型**：Why don't you...? / You should...
- **易混点**：Why don't you（你为什么不...，建议）vs Why do you（你为什么...，询问原因）
- **口诀**："Why don't you你为什么不should应该argue争论communicate交流parents父母"

## Unit 5 What were you doing when the rainstorm came?
- **核心句型**：What were you doing when...?
- **易混点**：过去进行时（was/were+doing）vs 一般过去时（did）
- **口诀**："What were you doing你正在做什么rainstorm暴风雨suddenly突然silence沉默"

## Unit 6 An old man tried to move the mountains.
- **核心句型**：What happened? / How did it start?
- **易混点**：tried to（试图）vs tried doing（尝试）
- **口诀**："tried to试图move移动mountains山as soon as一...就so...that如此...以至于unless除非"

## Unit 7 What's the highest mountain in the world?
- **核心句型**：What's the highest... in the world?
- **易混点**：highest（最高的）vs higher（更高的）
- **口诀**："What's the highest最高的world世界desert沙漠population人口ancient古代的wide宽的"

## Unit 8 Have you read Treasure Island yet?
- **核心句型**：Have you read... yet?
- **易混点**：Have you read（你读过...吗？，现在完成时）vs Did you read（你读过...吗？，过去时）
- **口诀**："Have you read你读过...吗yet已经Treasure Island金银岛"

## Unit 9 Have you ever been to a museum?
- **核心句型**：Have you ever been to...?
- **易混点**：been to（去过）vs gone to（去了，还没回来）
- **口诀**："Have you ever been to你曾经去过...吗museum博物馆amusement park游乐园"

## Unit 10 I've had this bike for three years.
- **核心句型**：I've had... for/since...
- **易混点**：for+时间段（持续了多久）vs since+时间点（从什么时候开始）
- **口诀**："I've had我拥有了for三年since 2020年for时间段since时间点"
