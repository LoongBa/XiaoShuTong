# 初中七年级 英语 知识卡片（背景钩子）

> 用途：背诵时展示的记忆增强卡片，用关键词锚定 + 易混点对比 + 口诀辅助精细复述
> 展示时机：✕ 阶段学习新知识点时展示；△○★ 复习时默认隐藏，卡壳时由 AI 导师按需提示
> 设计原则：正面问题化、关键词锚定、层级递进、易混点配对

# ===== 七年级上册 =====

## Unit 1 My name's Gina.
- **核心句型**：What's your name? / My name is... / I'm...
- **易混点**：What's your name?（你叫什么名字？）vs What's your phone number?（你的电话号码是多少？）
- **口诀**："name名字phone number电话号码，自我介绍My name is/I'm"

## Unit 2 This is my sister.
- **核心句型**：This is my... / These/Those are... / Who's that man/woman?
- **易混点**：This is（单数）vs These are（复数）、That is（单数）vs Those are（复数）
- **口诀**："This/That单数These/Those复数，介绍家人用This is my..."

## Unit 3 Is this your pencil?
- **核心句型**：Is this your...? / Are those your...?
- **易混点**：Is this（单数疑问）vs Are those（复数疑问）
- **口诀**："Is this单数疑问Are those复数疑问，确认物品用一般疑问句"

## Unit 4 Where's my schoolbag?
- **核心句型**：Where is my...? / It's on/in/under...
- **易混点**：on（在...上面）vs in（在...里面）vs under（在...下面）
- **口诀**："Where问在哪里on上面in里面under下面"

## Unit 5 Do you have a soccer ball?
- **核心句型**：Do you have a...? / Let's...
- **易混点**：Do you have（你有吗？）vs Can you（你会吗？）
- **口诀**："Do you have你有吗Let's让我们，运动单词soccer ball basketball"

## Unit 6 Do you like bananas?
- **核心句型**：Do you like...? / I like/don't like...
- **易混点**：Do you like（你喜欢吗？）vs Would you like（你想要吗？）
- **口诀**："Do you like你喜欢吗I like我喜欢，水果食物单词要记牢"

## Unit 7 How much are these socks?
- **核心句型**：How much is/are...?
- **易混点**：How much is（单数多少钱）vs How much are（复数多少钱）
- **口诀**："How much问多少钱is单数are复数，衣物单词socks sweater shoes"

## Unit 8 When is your birthday?
- **核心句型**：When is your birthday? / How old are you?
- **易混点**：When（什么时候）vs How old（多大年纪）
- **口诀**："When问时间birthday生日How old问年龄，月份单词要记全"

## Unit 9 My favorite subject is science.
- **核心句型**：What's your favorite subject? / My favorite subject is...
- **易混点**：favorite（最喜欢的）vs like（喜欢）
- **口诀**："favorite最喜欢subject科目，science科学math数学music音乐"

# ===== 七年级下册 =====

## Unit 1 Can you play the guitar?
- **核心句型**：Can you...? / What club do you want to join?
- **易混点**：Can you（你会吗？）vs Can I（我能吗？）
- **口诀**："Can you你会吗guitar吉他swim游泳sing唱歌"

## Unit 2 What time do you go to school?
- **核心句型**：What time do you...?
- **易混点**：What time（几点）vs When（什么时候）
- **口诀**："What time问具体时间get up起床go to school上学"

## Unit 3 How do you get to school?
- **核心句型**：How do you get to...?
- **易混点**：How（怎样）vs How long（多长时间）vs How far（多远）
- **口诀**："How问方式bus公共汽车bike自行车walk步行"

## Unit 4 Don't eat in class.
- **核心句型**：Don't... / You must/can't...
- **易混点**：Don't（不要，祈使句否定）vs You can't（你不能，能力/许可否定）
- **口诀**："Don't不要rules规则must必须can't不能"

## Unit 5 Why do you like pandas?
- **核心句型**：Why do you like...? / Because...
- **易混点**：Why（为什么）vs Because（因为）
- **口诀**："Why问原因Because回答panda熊猫cute可爱"

## Unit 6 I'm watching TV.
- **核心句型**：What are you doing? / I'm/He's/She's...
- **易混点**：现在进行时（be+doing）vs 一般现在时（do/does）
- **口诀**："What are you doing你正在做什么be+doing现在进行时"

## Unit 7 It's raining!
- **核心句型**：How's the weather? / It's...
- **易混点**：How's the weather?（天气怎么样？）vs What's the weather like?（天气怎么样？）
- **口诀**："How's the weather问天气raining下雨windy有风sunny晴朗"

## Unit 8 Is there a post office near here?
- **核心句型**：Is there a... near here? / ...is next to...
- **易混点**：Is there（有吗？，单数）vs Are there（有吗？，复数）
- **口诀**："Is there单数有吗post office邮局hospital医院next to旁边"

## Unit 9 What does he look like?
- **核心句型**：What does he/she look like? / He/She is...
- **易混点**：What does he look like?（他长什么样？）vs What does he like?（他喜欢什么？）
- **口诀**："look like长什么样tall高thin瘦curly卷发straight直发"

## Unit 10 I'd like some noodles.
- **核心句型**：What kind of... would you like? / I'd like...
- **易混点**：I'd like（我想要）vs I like（我喜欢）
- **口诀**："I'd like我想要would like想要noodles面条beef牛肉"

## Unit 11 How was your school trip?
- **核心句型**：How was...? / What did you do?
- **易混点**：How was（...怎么样？，过去时）vs How is（...怎么样？，现在时）
- **口诀**："How was过去怎么样school trip学校旅行went去had有"

## Unit 12 What did you do last weekend?
- **核心句型**：What did you do last weekend? / I went to...
- **易混点**：did（助动词过去式）vs do（助动词原形）
- **口诀**："What did you do你做了什么last weekend上个周末went went cinema电影"
