## 一、八年级下册教材结构（8 主题单元）

| Unit | 主题 | Big Question | 核心句型 | 语法焦点 |
|------|------|-------------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Time to Relax | Why are free-time activities important? | How do you spend your free time? / I exercise to stay healthy. | 不定式(目的/宾补) |
| 2 | Stay Healthy | How do we take care of ourselves? | You should... / What's wrong? I have a headache. | should/could 建议；反身代词 |
| 3 | Growing Up | How do we deal with emotions? | What makes you upset? / Although... | 连接词 although/until/so that |
| 4 | The Wonders of Nature | How do we connect with nature? | It is the highest mountain. / Why do we explore nature? | 比较级最高级；大数 |
| 5 | Nature's Temper | How do natural disasters affect us? | What were you doing when...? / The typhoon hit. | 过去进行时 |
| 6 | A Good Read | Why should we read great books? | Have you ever read? / I've already read... | 现在完成时(already/yet) |
| 7 | Crossing Cultures | How do we communicate across cultures? | How different are our cultures? / since & for | 现在完成时(since/for) |
| 8 | Making a Difference | Why should we help others? | What can we do to help? / I have volunteered... | 现在完成时应用 |
