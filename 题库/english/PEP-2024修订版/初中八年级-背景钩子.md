# 初中八年级 英语 背景钩子（知识卡片）

> 用途：背诵时展示的记忆增强卡片，核心句型锚定 + 易混点对比 + 记忆钩子
> 展示时机：✕ 学习新单元句型时展示；△○★ 复习时隐藏，卡壳时按需提示
> 版本：2026-09 对照 2024 修订新版教材（8 主题单元）

---

## Unit 1 Happy Holiday（快乐假期）
- **核心句型**：How was your holiday? / What did you do? / Did you go anywhere interesting?
- **关键词**：beach, holiday, travel, interesting, fantastic
- **记忆点**：一般过去时 + 不定代词
- **易混点**：anyone/anything（任何）vs someone/something（某）；"take my breath away"（令人惊叹）
- **记忆钩子**："Did you go anywhere interesting?"——假期经历不定代词

## Unit 2 Home Sweet Home（甜蜜的家）
- **核心句型**：Can/Could you please...? / Would you like to...?
- **关键词**：mop, clean, water the plants, invite, tidy
- **记忆点**：情态动词礼貌请求
- **易混点**：can（一般）vs could（更礼貌）
- **记忆钩子**："Could you please...?"——礼貌求助

## Unit 3 Same or Different?（异同）
- **核心句型**：She is funnier than me. / I work as hard as her.
- **关键词**：similar, different, taller, lazier, team
- **记忆点**：形容词比较级 + as...as
- **易混点**：-er（taller) vs more+adj（more interesting）；as...as（同级）
- **记忆钩子**："twins look similar"—比较级问差异

## Unit 4 Amazing Plants and Animals（奇妙的动植物）
- **核心句型**：Bamboo is used for... / What is it used for?
- **关键词**：amazing, bamboo, useful, forest
- **记忆点**：被动语态 is used / is made
- **易混点**：be used for（用途）vs be used to（习惯）
- **记忆钩子**："is used for making paper"—用途被动句

## Unit 5 What a Delicious Meal!（美食）
- **核心句型**：How do you make it? / First..., Then..., Finally...
- **关键词**：cook, boil, sauce, delicious, recipe
- **记忆点**：顺序副词表过程
- **易混点**：delicious（美味）vs sweet/spicy（具体味道）；"in the first place"≠ recipe 的 first
- **记忆钩子**："First boil, then add, finally serve"—做菜流程

## Unit 6 Plan for Yourself（自我规划）
- **核心句型**：I would like to be an engineer. / I plan to exercise daily.
- **关键词**：plan, dream, engineer, healthy, future
- **记忆点**：would like to + be going to
- **易混点**：would like（意愿）vs be going to（计划）
- **记忆钩子**："My plan is to...—未来规划

## Unit 7 When Tomorrow Comes（未来降临）
- **核心句型**：Robots will help us. / I will live in a bigger house.
- **关键词**：future, robot, will, predict, change
- **记忆点**：一般将来时 will + 动词原形
- **易混点**：will（预测）vs be going to（计划）
- **记忆钩子**："will" 预测未来生活

## Unit 8 Let's Communicate!（让我们沟通）
- **核心句型**：We should listen carefully. / Would you like to...?
- **关键词**：communicate, listen, politely, invitation, idea
- **记忆点**：建议与邀请句型
- **易混点**：Let's...（提议）vs Shall we...?（建议）
- **记忆钩子**："Would you like to...?"—礼貌邀请沟通
