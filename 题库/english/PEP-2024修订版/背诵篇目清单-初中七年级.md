## 一、教材结构

### 七年级上册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Starter Unit 1-3 | 问候/颜色/物品 | hello, good morning, what's this | Good morning! What's this? |
| Unit 1 My name's Gina. | 自我介绍 | name, phone number, first/last name | What's your name? What's your phone number? |
| Unit 2 This is my sister. | 家庭成员 | sister, brother, mother, father, parent | This/That is... These/Those are... |
| Unit 3 Is this your pencil? | 学习用品 | pencil, book, eraser, box, schoolbag | Is this/that your...? |
| Unit 4 Where's my schoolbag? | 位置 | table, bed, bookcase, sofa, chair, under, on, in | Where is/are...? |
| Unit 5 Do you have a soccer ball? | 球类运动 | have, soccer, basketball, tennis, volleyball | Do you have a...? Let's... |
| Unit 6 Do you like bananas? | 食物 | banana, hamburger, tomato, ice-cream, salad | Do you like...? I like/don't like... |
| Unit 7 How much are these socks? | 价格衣物 | much, sock, T-shirt, shorts, sweater, shoes | How much is/are...? |
| Unit 8 When is your birthday? | 日期节日 | January~December, birthday, party, trip | When is...? How old are you? |
| Unit 9 My favorite subject is science. | 学科 | science, math, music, Chinese, geography | What's your favorite subject? Why? |

### 七年级下册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 Can you play the guitar? | 能力俱乐部 | swim, sing, dance, draw, play chess, guitar | Can you...? What club do you want to join? |
| Unit 2 What time do you go to school? | 日常作息 | get up, go to school, eat breakfast, go to bed | What time do you...? |
| Unit 3 How do you get to school? | 交通方式 | take the bus, ride a bike, walk, subway, train | How do you get to...? |
| Unit 4 Don't eat in class. | 规章制度 | rules, arrive late, listen to music, be quiet | Don't... You must/can't... |
| Unit 5 Why do you like pandas? | 动物描述 | cute, lazy, smart, beautiful, scary | Why do you like...? Because... |
| Unit 6 I'm watching TV. | 现在进行时 | doing homework, reading, eating, playing | What are you doing? I'm/He's/She's... |
| Unit 7 It's raining! | 天气描述 | raining, windy, cloudy, sunny, snowing | How's the weather? It's... |
| Unit 8 Is there a post office near here? | 位置问路 | post office, police station, hospital, hotel | Is there a... near here? |
| Unit 9 What does he look like? | 外貌描述 | tall, short, thin, heavy, curly, straight | What does he/she look like? |
| Unit 10 I'd like some noodles. | 食物点餐 | beef, mutton, cabbage, potato, medium, large | What kind of... would you like? |
| Unit 11 How was your school trip? | 过去时描述 | went for a walk, milked cows, rode horses, fed chickens | How was...? What did you do? |
| Unit 12 What did you do last weekend? | 过去时活动 | did my homework, went to the cinema, played badminton | What did you do last weekend? |
