# 初中七年级 英语 背诵篇目清单

> 来源：教材原文复核（2026-09 对照《义务教育教科书·英语七年级下册》2024 修订新版全文重写）
> 基准版本：人教 PEP 2024 修订新版（基于《义务教育英语课程标准（2022年版）》编写，主题式单元体系）
> 用途：题库建设篇目元信息（L0 层）
> 可靠度：🟢 高（直接对照教材全文）
> 说明：旧版清单为 Go For It! 旧结构（Unit1 Can you play the guitar? 等 12 单元），2024 新版为 8 个主题式单元，已整体替换

## 一、教材结构（8 主题单元）

| Unit | 主题 | Big Question | 核心句型（Section A/B） | 语法焦点 | 记忆点 |
|------|------|-------------|------------------------|---------|--------|
| Unit 1 | Animal Friends | Why are animals important? | What's your favourite animal? / Why do you like...? — Because... / Where are...from? / What do they eat? | Wh-questions; Adjectives; Plurals | ~15 |
| Unit 2 | No Rules, No Order | Why do we need rules? | What rules do we have? / We must/mustn't... / We can/can't... / Don't... / We have to... | Imperatives; Modal verbs (can/have to/must) | ~14 |
| Unit 3 | Keep Fit | How do we keep fit? | Is it yours? No, it isn't mine. / How often do you play? | Possessive pronouns; Adverbs of frequency | ~13 |
| Unit 4 | Eat Well | How do we eat well? | What would you like to eat? / Would you like A or B? / I'd like... | Alternative questions; Countable/uncountable nouns | ~13 |
| Unit 5 | Here and Now | What brings people together? | What are you doing right now? / I'm doing... / This is...speaking. / Could I speak to...? | Present continuous tense (1) | ~14 |
| Unit 6 | Rain or Shine | How does the weather affect us? | What's the weather like? / It's raining/sunny... / What are you doing? | Present continuous tense (2) | ~13 |
| Unit 7 | A Day to Remember | What makes a day special? | How was your school trip? / What did you see/do? / We went to... | Simple past tense (1) | ~14 |
| Unit 8 | Once upon a Time | Why do we tell stories? | Once upon a time... / Did you like the story? / 过去式讲故事 | Simple past tense (2) | ~13 |

| Unit | 主题 | 背诵要求 | 记忆点 |

| 2 | No Rules | 较高（规则情态动词） | ~14 |

| 6 | Rain or Shine | 中等（天气+进行时） | ~13 |
