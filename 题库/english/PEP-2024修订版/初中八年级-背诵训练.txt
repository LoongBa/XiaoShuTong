# 初中八年级 英语 背诵训练题库（分阶检索法）
# 教材：人教PEP 2024 修订新版（8 主题单元）
# 题型：S1 正向（中文→英文）/ S2 逆向（英文→中文·用途）/ S3 语法要点 / S4 综合
# 格式：题目###答案（# 开头为注释）

# ===== 八年级上册 =====

## Unit 1 Happy Holiday
# S1 正向
询问"假期过得怎样"？###How was your holiday? — It was fantastic.
问"你去过什么有趣的地方吗"？###Did you go anywhere interesting?
# S2 逆向
"You went to Yunnan with my family." 是什么时态？###一般过去时（假期经历）
"took my breath away" 意思？###令人惊叹 / 美得让人窒息
# S3 语法要点
默写不定代词？###someone, anyone, everyone, nothing, something, anything, everything
# S4 综合
默写"分享假期经历"？###A: How was your holiday? B: It was great! I went to Yunnan. I saw the mountains and ate nice food.

## Unit 2 Home Sweet Home
# S1 正向
请求帮忙？###Can you help me clean the room?
"你想拖地还是擦窗"？###Would you like to mop the floor or clean the windows?
# S2 逆向
"Could you please...?" 用于？###礼貌请求帮助
# S3 语法要点
情态动词 can/could 的礼貌请求？###Could you please + 动词原形? — Sure / No problem.
# S4 综合
默写"为客人到来做准备"的分工？###A: Can you help me clean up for grandma's visit? B: Sure. I'd like to mop the floor. C: I'll water the plants.

## Unit 3 Same or Different?
# S1 正向
比较两个人？###She is funnier than me. / He is taller than I.
# S2 逆向
"as...as" 表示什么？###"和……一样"（as tall as）
# S3 语法要点
形容词比较级构成？###单音节 -er；多音节 more + adj.（taller, more interesting）
# S4 综合
默写"比较双胞胎差异"？###A: How are you different? B: I'm taller than my sister, but she is nicer than me.

## Unit 4 Amazing Plants and Animals
# S1 正向
描述竹子？###Bamboo grows very fast and is used for many things.
问"什么让生物奇妙"？###What makes plants and animals amazing?
# S2 逆向
"is used for" 表示？###被用来做某事（被动语态）
# S3 语法要点
一般现在时 + 被动结构？###主 + am/is/are + 过去分词（is used, is made）
# S4 综合
描述一种动物？###The elephant is huge and strong. It is friendly to people and is used to carry things.

## Unit 5 What a Delicious Meal
# S1 正向
问"怎么做"？###How do you make it? — First boil the noodles...
用顺序词？###First, Then, Next, After that, Finally
# S2 逆向
"first → finally" 是什么？###顺序副词（过程描述）
# S3 语法要点
叙述过程的顺序副词？###（叙述流程与动作的顺序连贯）
# S4 综合
描述一道菜的做法？###First I boil the noodles. Then I add the sauce and vegetables. Finally I serve.

## Unit 6 Plan for Yourself
# S1 正向
谈未来理想？###I would like to be an engineer in the future.
# S2 综合
"want to be / would like to be" 区别？###想要成为（将来）
# S3 语法要点
would like to do / be going to？###would like 表意愿；be going to 表计划
# S4 综合
为未来做计划？###My plan is to exercise every day. I would like to be healthy and strong.

## Unit 7 When Tomorrow Comes
# S1 正向
预测未来 will？###In the future, robots will help us do housework.
# S2 逆向
"will" 后接什么？###动词原形
# S3 语法要点
一般将来时 will+动词原形？###主语 + will + 动词原形（won't 否定）
# S4 综合
谈论 20 年后的生活？###In 20 years, I will live in a bigger city. Maybe I will have a robot.

## Unit 8 Let's Communicate
# S1 正向
提建议 Let's / should？###We should listen carefully. / You'd better speak clearly.
# S2 逆向
"Would you like...?" 用于？###礼貌邀请
# S3 语法要点
建议、邀请的句型？###Let's..., Shall we...?, Would you like...?
# S4 综合
礼貌邀请与接受？###A: Would you like to have dinner with us? B: Yes, I'd love to. / Thank you!
