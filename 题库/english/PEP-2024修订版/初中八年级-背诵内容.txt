# 初中八年级 英语 背诵内容测试题
# 教材：人教PEP版 2024 修订新版（主题式单元，8 单元）
# 格式：问题###答案（# 开头为注释）
# 说明：每单元核心句型 + 语法焦点，对照新版教材

# ===== 八年级上册 =====

# Unit 1 Happy Holiday（快乐假期）
询问"你假期过得怎么样"并回答？###How was your holiday? — It was fantastic / great.
问"你做了什么"（过去）？###What did you do? — I went to Yunnan with my family.
一般疑问"你去过什么有趣的地方吗"？###Did you go anywhere interesting? / Did you visit Yulong Mountain?
描述假期感受？###It just took my breath away. / We enjoyed it very much.
用不定代词表"某人/任何事"？###Someone said... / Did you eat anything special? — Yes, everything tasted good.

# Unit 2 Home Sweet Home（甜蜜的家）
请求帮助做家务？###Can/Could you please help me clean the room? — Sure / No problem.
用 would like 表达选择？###Would you like to mop the floor or clean the windows? — I'd like to clean the windows.
邀请朋友来家里？###Can I invite my friends to our new house?
家务动词短语？###mop the floor, clean the windows, water the plants, put the books on the wall

# Unit 3 Same or Different?（异同）
用比较级比较某人？###She is funnier than me. / I'm a little taller than Ella.
"问今年有何不同"？###How is this year different? — It's more exciting than last year.
用 as...as 表"如同"？###I work as hard as her. / They look very similar.
调侃"他比我懒"？###He is lazier than me, but that's not true!

# Unit 4 Amazing Plants and Animals（奇妙的动植物）
描述动植物的特征？###It is very tall / thick. The leaves are big and green.
用被动/一般现在描述？###Bamboo is used for many things. / The elephant is a great animal.
问"它为何惊人"？###What makes plants and animals amazing? — They are useful to us.

# Unit 5 What a Delicious Meal!（美食）
用顺序词描述做菜步骤？###First, ... Then, ... Next, ... After that, ... Finally, ...
问"怎么做这道菜"？###How do you make / cook it? — Boil the noodles, then add the sauce.
描述食物口感？###It's delicious / tasty / sweet / spicy.

# Unit 6 Plan for Yourself（自我规划）
谈论未来计划（would like）？###I would like to be an engineer / doctor in the future.
制订计划？###My plan is to get up early and exercise every day.
问"你将来想做什么"？###What do you want / dream to be in the future?

# Unit 7 When Tomorrow Comes（未来）
用 will 预测未来？###I will / won't ... In the future, robots will help us.
谈论未来生活变化？###In 20 years, I will live in a bigger house. / I don't know for sure.
表达不确定/担心？###I'm not sure. Maybe it will be better. / Nothing is for certain.

# Unit 8 Let's Communicate!（交流沟通）
问"怎么沟通"？###What makes good communication? — Listening and speaking politely.
表达建议？###We should listen carefully / You'd better speak clearly.
礼貌地邀请与回应？###Would you like to...? / That's a great idea.
