## 一、教材结构

### 三年级上册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 Hello! | 问候与自我介绍 | hello, hi, goodbye, I'm... | Hello! I'm... |
| Unit 2 Colours | 颜色 | red, yellow, green, blue | What colour is it? |
| Unit 3 Look at me! | 身体部位 | head, eye, ear, nose, mouth | Look at me! |
| Unit 4 We love animals | 动物 | cat, dog, bird, fish, monkey | I have a... |
| Unit 5 Let's eat! | 食物 | cake, bread, egg, milk, water | Can I have some...? |
| Unit 6 Happy birthday! | 数字1-10 | one~ten | How old are you? |

### 三年级下册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 Welcome back to school! | 国家 | UK, Canada, USA, China | Where are you from? |
| Unit 2 My family | 家庭成员 | father, mother, brother, sister | Who's that man/woman? |
| Unit 3 At the zoo | 动物特征 | tall, short, thin, fat, big, small | It's so... |
| Unit 4 Where is my car? | 位置 | in, on, under, desk, chair | Where is my...? |
| Unit 5 Do you like pears? | 水果 | apple, pear, orange, banana | Do you like...? |
| Unit 6 How many? | 数字11-20 | eleven~twenty | How many... do you see/have? |
