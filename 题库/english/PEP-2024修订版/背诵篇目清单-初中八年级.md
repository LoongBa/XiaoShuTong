## 一、教材结构

### 八年级上册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 Where did you go on vacation? | 度假经历 | went to the beach, visited museums, stayed at home | Where did you go on vacation? |
| Unit 2 How often do you exercise? | 频率活动 | always, usually, often, sometimes, hardly ever | How often do you...? |
| Unit 3 I'm more outgoing than my sister. | 比较级 | more outgoing, funnier, more serious, quieter | Is/Are... more... than...? |
| Unit 4 What's the best movie theater? | 最高级 | best, worst, most comfortable, closest | What's the best...? |
| Unit 5 Do you want to watch a game show? | 节目类型 | talk show, soap opera, sports show, news | Do you want to watch...? |
| Unit 6 I'm going to study computer science. | 打算计划 | grow up, computer science, medicine, engineer | What are you going to be? |
| Unit 7 Will people have robots? | 将来时 | will, robot, fewer, less, more | Will there be...? Will you...? |
| Unit 8 How do you make a banana milkshake? | 制作步骤 | turn on, cut up, pour...into, add...to | How do you make a...? |
| Unit 9 Can you come to my party? | 邀请拒绝 | come to the party, go to the dentist, prepare for | Can you come to...? |
| Unit 10 If you go to the party, you'll have a great time! | 条件句 | if, have a great time, organize, do half of | If you...will you...? |

### 八年级下册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 What's the matter? | 健康问题 | have a cold, stomachache, headache, toothache | What's the matter? What should I do? |
| Unit 2 I'll help to clean up the city parks. | 志愿服务 | clean up, give out, volunteer, put off | I'll... You could... |
| Unit 3 Could you please clean your room? | 礼貌请求 | do the dishes, take out the rubbish, fold the clothes | Could you please...? |
| Unit 4 Why don't you talk to your parents? | 建议 | allow, argue, get on with, communicate | Why don't you...? You should... |
| Unit 5 What were you doing when the rainstorm came? | 过去进行时 | at the time of, suddenly, in silence, take down | What were you doing when...? |
| Unit 6 An old man tried to move the mountains. | 故事复述 | as soon as, so...that, unless, turn...into | What happened? How did it start? |
| Unit 7 What's the highest mountain in the world? | 比较级最高级 | deep, desert, population, ancient, wide | What's the highest/deepest/longest...? |
| Unit 8 Have you read Treasure Island yet? | 现在完成时 | have read, have been to, have gone to | Have you ever...? |
| Unit 9 Have you ever been to a museum? | 体验经历 | space museum, amusement park, thousand | Have you ever been to...? |
| Unit 10 I've had this bike for three years. | 现在完成时 | have had, have owned, since, for | How long have you had...? |
