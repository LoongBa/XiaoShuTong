## 一、八年级上册教材结构（8 主题单元）

| Unit | 主题 | Big Question | 核心句型 | 语法焦点 |
|------|------|-------------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Happy Holiday | What makes a great holiday? | How was your holiday? / What did you do? / Did you go anywhere interesting? | 一般过去时；不定代词 |
| 2 | Home Sweet Home | What does home mean to you? | Can/Could you please...? / Would you like to...? | 情态动词(请求) |
| 3 | Same or Different? | How do we compare with each other? | She is funnier than me. / I work as hard as her. | 形容词比较级；as...as |
| 4 | Amazing Plants and Animals | What makes them amazing? | Bamboo is used for... / What is it used for? | 被动语态；一般现在 |
| 5 | What a Delicious Meal! | Why do we learn to cook? | First..., Then..., Finally... / How do you make it? | 顺序副词 |
| 6 | Plan for Yourself | Why do we need plans? | I would like to be... / I plan to... | would like；be going to |
| 7 | When Tomorrow Comes | What will the future look like? | Robots will help us. / I will live... | 一般将来时 will |
| 8 | Let's Communicate! | What makes good communication? | We should listen... / Would you like to...? | 建议、邀请句型 |

## 三、旧版对照

旧版 Go For It 八上（Unit1-10）与 2024 新版：
- 旧 Unit 1/vacation → 新版 Happy Holiday（保留过去时询问假期，新增不定代词）
- 旧 Unit 3 比较级 → 新版 Same or Different（主题一致）
- 全新主题单元：Home Sweet Home / Amazing Plants & Animals / Delicious Meal / Plan / Future / Communication
- 新版强调主题式语言功能，非逐句翻译
