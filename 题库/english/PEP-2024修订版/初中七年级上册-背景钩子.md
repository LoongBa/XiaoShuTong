# ===== Starter Unit 1 Welcome! =====
## Starter Unit 1 Welcome!（欢迎）
- **核心句型**：Good morning. / How are you? / What's your name? / Nice to meet you.
- **关键词**：hello, hi, goodbye, fine, thanks, meet
- **记忆点**：be 动词第一人称介绍
- **易混点**：What's your name?（问姓名）vs How are you?（问近况）
- **记忆钩子**："Nice to meet you, too!"（回应礼貌介绍）

# ===== Starter Unit 2 Keep Tidy! =====
## Starter Unit 2 Keep Tidy!（保持整洁）
- **核心句型**：What do you have...? What colour is/are...?
- **关键词**：schoolbag, cap, ruler, pencil, book
- **记忆点**：there be / have 表达拥有
- **易混点**：have（拥有）vs there be（存在）
- **记忆钩子**："Keep tidy"——整理书包问颜色

# ===== Starter Unit 3 My Yard =====
## Starter Unit 3 My Yard（我的院子）
- **核心句型**：What's this? What are these/those? How many...?
- **关键词**：rabbit, duck, yard, farm, how many
- **记忆点**：单复数 + 数量
- **易混点**：this/these（近/复数）vs that/those（远/复数）
- **记忆钩子**："How many rabbits?"——count 数鸭子

# ===== Unit 1 You and Me =====
## Unit 1 You and Me（你和我）
- **核心句型**：How are you? / This is my friend. / What class are you in? / Who's your class teacher?
- **关键词**：friend, classmate, teacher, class, from
- **记忆点**：be 动词介绍 + nationality
- **易混点**：first name（名）vs last name（姓）
- **记忆钩子**："He's from the US."——自我介绍国籍

# ===== Unit 2 We're Family! =====
## Unit 2 We're Family!（一家人）
- **核心句型**：This is my family photo. What does he look like?
- **关键词**：family, parents, sister, brother, tall, kind
- **记忆点**：形容词表 + 所有格
- **易混点**：my（我的adj）vs mine（我的noun）
- **记忆钩子**："Family"——描述家人的长相与性格

# ===== Unit 3 My School =====
## Unit 3 My School（我的学校）
- **核心句型**：There is/are... Where is...? + 方位介词
- **关键词**：library, gym, classroom, playground, behind
- **记忆点**：There be + 方位描述
- **易混点**：in front of（前面）vs in the front of（前部）
- **记忆钩子**："Where's the library?" "It's next to the gym."——逛校园

# ===== Unit 4 My Favourite Subject =====
## Unit 4 My Favourite Subject（我最爱的科目）
- **核心句型**：What's your favourite subject? Why? Because...
- **关键词**：subject, English, math, science, difficult, interesting
- **记忆点**：连词 and/but/because
- **易混点**：Why（问原因）→Because 回答；favourite 英式
- **记忆钩子**："I like science because it's fun."——学科喜好与理由

# ===== Unit 5 Fun Clubs =====
## Unit 5 Fun Clubs（有趣的俱乐部）
- **关键词**：guitar, swim, sing, dance, join, can
- **记忆点**：能力情态动词 can
- **易混点**：can（能力）vs 一般行为
- **记忆钩子**："Can you play the guitar?" "Yes, I can."——校园招新

# ===== Unit 6 A Day in the Life =====
## Unit 6 A Day in the Life（一天的生活）
- **核心句型**：What time do you ...? I get up at...
- **关键词**：get up, go to school, breakfast, time, routine
- **记忆点**：时间表达 + 日常作息
- **易混点**：at（时刻）on（星期/in（月/时段）
- **记忆钩子**："What time do you get up?" "At 7"—作息询问

# ===== Unit 7 Happy Birthday! =====
## Unit 7 Happy Birthday!（生日快乐）
- **核心句型**：When is your birthday? How old are you?
- **关键词**：birthday, date, party, month, present
- **记忆点**：序数词表达日期
- **易混点**：询问月份（When...）vs 年龄（How old...）
- **记忆钩子**："My birthday is on June 1st."——生日日期顺序词
