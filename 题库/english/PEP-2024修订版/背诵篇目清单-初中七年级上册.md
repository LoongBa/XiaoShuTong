# 初中七年级上册 英语 背诵篇目清单

> 来源：教材原文复核（2026-09 对照《义务教育教科书·英语七年级上册》2024 修订新版）
> 基准版本：人教 PEP 2024 修订新版（基于2022课标，主题式单元）
> 用途：题库建设篇目元信息（L0 层）
> 说明：旧版为 Starter+Unit1-9（Go For It 旧结构），2024 新版为 Starter 1-3 + Unit 1-7 主题式，已更换

---

## 一、教材结构（Starter 1-3 + Unit 1-7 = 10 模块）

| 模块 | 主题 | Big Question | 核心句型 | 语法焦点 |
|------|------|-------------|----------|---------|
| Starter Unit 1 | Welcome! | How do you greet people? | Hello/Hi/Good morning. How are you? Nice to meet you. What's your name? 单元 | be 动词；人称代词 |
| Starter Unit 2 | Keep Tidy! | What do you have? | What do you have in your schoolbag? What colour is the cap? | 一般现在时；there be |
| Starter Unit 3 | My Yard(农场) | What is fun in a yard? | What's this/that? What are these/those? How many rabbits? | 单复数；how many |
| Unit 1 | You and Me | How do we make new friends? | How are you? I'm fine. This is my friend. He's from the US. What class are you in? Who's your class teacher? | be动词(am/is/are)；who's |
| Unit 2 | We're Family! | What does family mean to you? | This is my family photo. What does he look like? He's tall and thin. | 所有格('s)；形容词表 
| Unit 3 | My School | What do you like about your school? | There is/are... Where is the library? It's next to the classroom. | There be；方位介词 |
| Unit 4 | My Favourite Subject | Why do we learn subjects? | What's your favourite subject? Why? Because I like English. | 连词 and/but/because |
| Unit 5 | Fun Clubs | Why do we join clubs? | Can you play the guitar? Yes, I can. I'd like to join the club. | can(能力情态动词) |
| Unit 6 | A Day in the Life | How do you spend your school day? | What time do you get up? I get up at seven. When do you go to bed? | 时间表达；Wh-question 
| Unit 7 | Happy Birthday! | Why do we celebrate birthdays? | When is your birthday? It's on June 1st. How old are you? | 序数词；Wh-question 复习 |

## 二、背诵要求汇总

| 单元 | 主题 | 记忆点 | 背诵要求 |
|:----:|------|:------:|---------|
| Starter Unit 1-3 | 问候/物品/事物 | ~30 | 较高(基础句型) |
| Unit 1 | You and Me | 14 | 较高(自我介绍) |
| Unit 2 | Family | 13 | 较高(家庭介绍) |
| Unit 3 | My School | 15 | 较高(方位) |
| Unit 4 | Favourite Subject | 12 | 中等 |
| Unit 5 | Fun Clubs | 12 | 中等(can) |
| Unit 6 | A Day in the Life | 14 | 较高(时间) |
| Unit 7 | Happy Birthday | 13 | 中等(序数词) |
| **合计** | **10 模块** | **~126** | |

## 三、旧版对照说明

旧版 Go For It 七上（Starter + Unit 1-9）与 2024 新版：
- 保留：My name's Gina → You and Me；This is my sister → We're Family；be/have/状语基础句型
- 重组：旧 Unit 3-9 → 新版主题单元（My School/Favourite Subject/Fun Clubs/Birthday 等）
- 新增驱动：时间（Unit 6）、生日（Unit 7）等真实主题
- 新版强调"主题式语言 + 功能句型"而非逐句翻译背诵
