# 初中九年级上册 英语 背景钩子（知识卡片）

> 用途：背诵时展示的记忆增强卡片，核心句型锚定 + 易混点对比 + 记忆钩子
> 版本：2026-09 对照 2024 修订新版（8 主题单元）

---

## Unit 1 The Changing World（变化的世界）
- **核心句型**：My hometown has changed a lot. / I used to..., but now...
- **关键词**：change, hometown, progress, development
- **记忆点**：过去时 vs 现在完成
- **易混点**：used to（过去）vs have+过去分词（已变）
- **记忆钩子**："Change"——今昔对比用完成时

## Unit 2 Inspiring People（激励他人）
- **核心句型**：Who do you admire? / It is kind of him to help.
- **关键词**：admire, inspire, scientist, kind
- **记忆点**：It is...(of/for sb) to do
- **易混点**：of sb（人的品质）vs for sb（某事）
- **记忆钩子**：seed scientist—敬佩之人品质

## Unit 3 Smart Learning（智慧学习）
- **核心句型**：I think that... / I don't know if/whether...
- **关键词**：learn, method, practice, review, active
- **记忆点**：宾语从句 that/if/whether
- **易混点**：that(陈述) vs if/whether(是否)
- **记忆钩子**："I believe that practice helps"—宾语从句

## Unit 4 Our Memory（我们的记忆）
- **核心句型**：Do you know what helps memory? / I wonder why...
- **关键词**：memory, remember, forget, repeat
- **记忆点**：疑问词宾语从句
- **易混点**：what（什么）vs why（为何）引导
- **记忆钩子**："wonder why"—记忆方法疑问

## Unit 5 Power of Ideas（思想之力）
- **核心句型**：The telephone was invented in 1876.
- **关键词**：invent, invention, used for, useful
- **记忆点**：被动语态
- **易混点**：invented（主动/发明）vs was invented（被动）
- **记忆钩子**："was invented in"—发明时间

## Unit 6 Beyond Earth（太空之外）
- **核心句型**：Robots will be sent to Mars.
- **关键词**：space, Mars, rocket, explore, station
- **记忆点**：一般将来被动 will be done
- **易混点**：主动 will send vs 被动 will be sent
- **记忆钩子**："will be sent"—未来被动

## Unit 7 Feel the Rhythm（感受节奏）
- **核心句型**：I like music which is relaxing.
- **关键词**：music, song, rhythm, lyric, relax
- **记忆点**：关系从句 that/which/who
- **易混点**：which/that（物）vs who（人）
- **记忆钩子**："music which calms me"—定语从句

## Unit 8 More than a Game（不只是比赛）
- **核心句型**：The players who play well inspire us.
- **关键词**：sport, game, team, spirit, inspire
- **记忆点**：主谓一致（单复数配合）
- **易混点**：team is（整体）vs team are（成员）
- **记忆钩子**：体育精神激励，主谓一致
