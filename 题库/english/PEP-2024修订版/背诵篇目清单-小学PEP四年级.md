## 一、教材结构

### 四年级上册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 My classroom | 教室 | classroom, window, door, picture, board | What's in the classroom? |
| Unit 2 My schoolbag | 书包 | Chinese book, English book, maths book | I have a new... |
| Unit 3 My friends | 朋友描述 | strong, thin, quiet, friendly, glasses | He/She has... |
| Unit 4 My home | 家 | bedroom, living room, kitchen, study, bathroom | Is she/he in the...? |
| Unit 5 Dinner's ready | 食物 | beef, chicken, noodles, soup, vegetables | What would you like? |
| Unit 6 Meet my family! | 职业 | doctor, nurse, farmer, driver | What's your father/mother's job? |

### 四年级下册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 My school | 学校场所 | first floor, second floor, music room, art room | Where is the...? |
| Unit 2 What time is it? | 时间 | breakfast, lunch, dinner, English class | What time is it? |
| Unit 3 Weather | 天气 | warm, cold, hot, cool, sunny, windy | What's the weather like? |
| Unit 4 At the farm | 农场 | tomato, potato, carrot, horse, cow, sheep | Are these/those...? |
| Unit 5 My clothes | 衣服 | clothes, pants, hat, dress, skirt, jacket | Whose coat/pants are these? |
| Unit 6 Shopping | 购物 | gloves, scarf, umbrella, sunglasses | Can I help you? How much is it? |
