# 初中九年级 英语 背诵内容测试题
# 教材：人教PEP版 2024 修订版（全一册）
# 格式：问题###答案（# 开头为注释）

# ===== 九年级全一册 =====

# Unit 1 How can we become good learners?
我怎样才能学好英语？用英语怎么说？###How can I learn English well?
你应该和小组一起学习用英语怎么说？###You should study with a group.
你应该向老师求助用英语怎么说？###You should ask the teacher for help.
# Unit 2 I think that mooncakes are delicious!
我认为月饼很好吃用英语怎么说？###I think that mooncakes are delicious.
我想知道是否...用英语怎么说？###I wonder if/whether...
# Unit 3 Could you please tell me where the restrooms are?
你能告诉我洗手间在哪里吗？用英语怎么说？###Could you please tell me where the restrooms are?
# Unit 4 I used to be afraid of the dark.
我过去害怕黑暗用英语怎么说？###I used to be afraid of the dark.
我过去不害怕黑暗用英语怎么说？###I didn't use to be afraid of the dark.
# Unit 5 What are the shirts made of?
这些衬衫是用什么做的？用英语怎么说？###What are the shirts made of?
它们是用棉布做的用英语怎么说？###They are made of cotton.
# Unit 6 When was it invented?
它是什么时候被发明的？用英语怎么说？###When was it invented?
它是被谁发明的？用英语怎么说？###Who was it invented by?
# Unit 7 Teenagers should be allowed to choose their own clothes.
青少年应该被允许选择自己的衣服用英语怎么说？###Teenagers should be allowed to choose their own clothes.
# Unit 8 It must belong to Carla.
它一定是卡拉的用英语怎么说？###It must belong to Carla.
它可能是...用英语怎么说？###It might be...
它不可能是...用英语怎么说？###It can't be...
# Unit 9 I like music that I can dance to.
我喜欢我能跟着跳舞的音乐用英语怎么说？###I like music that I can dance to.
# Unit 10 You're supposed to shake hands.
你应该握手用英语怎么说？###You're supposed to shake hands.
# Unit 11 Sad movies make me cry.
悲伤的电影让我哭泣用英语怎么说？###Sad movies make me cry.
# Unit 12 Life is full of the unexpected.
生活充满意外用英语怎么说？###Life is full of the unexpected.
到我起床的时候，我已经迟到了用英语怎么说？###By the time I got up, I had been late.
# Unit 13 We're trying to save the earth!
我们正在努力拯救地球用英语怎么说？###We're trying to save the earth!
# Unit 14 I remember meeting all of you in Grade 7.
我记得在七年级见过你们所有人用英语怎么说？###I remember meeting all of you in Grade 7.
我期待着...用英语怎么说？###I'm looking forward to...
