## 一、教材结构

### 六年级上册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 How can I get there? | 问路 | science museum, post office, bookstore, cinema | How can I get to...? |
| Unit 2 Ways to go to school | 交通方式 | by bus, by plane, by taxi, on foot | How do you come to school? |
| Unit 3 My weekend plan | 周末计划 | visit, film, trip, supermarket, dictionary | What are you going to do? |
| Unit 4 I have a pen pal | 爱好 | dancing, singing, reading stories, playing | What are your hobbies? |
| Unit 5 What does he do? | 职业 | fisherman, scientist, pilot, coach | What does he/she do? |
| Unit 6 How do you feel? | 情绪 | angry, afraid, sad, worried, happy | How do you feel? |

### 六年级下册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 How tall are you? | 比较级 | taller, shorter, longer, older, younger | How tall/heavy are you? |
| Unit 2 Last weekend | 过去时 | watched TV, washed clothes, cleaned room | What did you do last weekend? |
| Unit 3 Where did you go? | 过去时 | went camping, went fishing, rode a bike | Where did you go over the holiday? |
| Unit 4 Then and now | 变化对比 | before, now, years ago, at that time | There was/were... Now there is/are... |
