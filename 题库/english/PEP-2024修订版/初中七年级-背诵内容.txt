# 初中七年级 英语 背诵内容测试题
# 教材：人教PEP版 2024 修订新版（主题式单元）
# 格式：问题###答案（# 开头为注释）
# 说明：题材为每单元核心句型 + 语法焦点 + 主题表达，2026-09 对照教材全文重写

# ===== Unit 1 Animal Friends =====
# 核心句型：询问喜好/缘由/产地
询问"你最喜欢的动物是什么"的英文句型？###What's your favourite animal? — It's the monkey.
询问"你为什么喜欢猴子"并回答的句型？###Why do you like monkeys? — Because they're clever and funny.
询问"企鹅来自哪里"的句型？###Where are penguins from? — They're from Antarctica.
用形容词描述动物（可爱/聪明/吓人）？###They're cute / clever / scary. / It looks lovely / amazing.
描述动物能力（飞/游泳）的句型？###They can swim fast, but they can't fly like other birds.

# ===== Unit 2 No Rules, No Order =====
# 核心句型：规则情态动词 + 祈使句
用 can/can't 表达"可以/不可以做某事"？###We can drink water. / We can't eat snacks in class.
用 must/mustn't 表达"必须/禁止"？###We must keep quiet. / We mustn't make noise.
用 have to 表达"不得不"？###We have to turn off our mobile phones.
用祈使句表达规则？###Walk in the hallway. Don't run! / Be polite and treat one another with respect.
询问"学校里有什么规则"的句型？###What rules do we have at school?

# ===== Unit 3 Keep Fit =====
# 物主代词 + 频率副词
询问"这是你的吗"并回答的句型？###Is it yours? — No, it isn't mine. Maybe it's Yaming's.
询问"你多久打一次（球类）"的句型？###How often do you play? — I play twice a week.
频率副词从不/很少/有时/经常的英文？###never, seldom, sometimes, often, usually, always
表达"你/我/他的"名词性物主代词？###mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs
建议一起运动的句型？###We should all play doubles some time. / Let's keep fit together.

# ===== Unit 4 Eat Well =====
# 点餐 + 选择疑问句 + 可数不可数
服务员询问"你想吃什么"的句型？###What would you like to order? / Would you like rice or noodles?
用 would like 表达"我想要..."？###I'd like a hot tofu dish.
选择疑问句"A 还是 B"怎么问？###Would you like chicken soup or fish soup?
询问"有没有豆腐"的句型？###Do you have anything with tofu?
建议点菜的句型？###How about a chicken and cabbage salad? / What about Mapo tofu?

# ===== Unit 5 Here and Now =====
# 现在进行时 + 打电话用语
询问"你现在正在做什么"的句型？###What are you doing right now? — I'm doing my homework.
询问"他现在在做什么"的句型？###What is he doing at the moment? — He's watching a film.
一般疑问句"你正在做...吗"？###Are you making zongzi? — Yes, we are.
打电话时"喂，我是..."怎么说？###Hello, this is... speaking. / It's... calling.
请找某人接电话怎么说？###Could I speak to...? / Is... there, please? — Hold on, please.

# ===== Unit 6 Rain or Shine =====
# 天气 + 进行时
询问"天气怎么样"的两种句型？###What's the weather like? / How's the weather?
描述天气（下雨/晴朗/刮风/温暖）的句型？###It's raining / sunny / windy / warm / cold.
描述温度的表达？###Today, it's about 28°C (degrees).
问"外面的人/你正在做什么"的句型？###What are you doing now? Are you outside at the moment?
根据天气建议活动？###When it snows, we usually stay in. / Let's go swimming when it's hot.

# ===== Unit 7 A Day to Remember =====
# 一般过去时描述难忘一天
询问"学校旅行怎么样"的句型？###How was your school trip? — It was really interesting.
问"你看了/做了什么"（过去）？###What did you see / do? — We went to the theatre.
过去式的两种情况/-ed 怎么发音？###动词过去式 -ed：talked/t/、moved/d/、wanted/ɪd/
描述过去去了哪里的句型？###We went to a factory / zoo / museum / farm last week.
描述过去感受/评价，###Was it boring? — No, it was amazing! It was not dull.

# ===== Unit 8 Once upon a Time =====
# 讲故事的一般过去时
开头"从前/很久以前"怎么用英文起语？###Once upon a time, there was / lived an emperor...
问"你喜欢/听过这个故事吗"类似？###Did you like / understand the story? — Yes, I did.
讲故事叙述过去动作（came/wanted/said/decided/cried）—Once upon a time, two brothers came and lied to the emperor. Everyone started laughing.
询问故事内容（角色/题/结）？Who are the characters? / What happened at the end?
用过去时描述故事情节走线，###At first they went, then they piled the clothes, finally the boy told the truth.
