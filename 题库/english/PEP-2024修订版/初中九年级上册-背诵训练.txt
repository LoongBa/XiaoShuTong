# 初中九年级 英语 背诵训练题库（分阶检索法）
# 教材：人教PEP 2024 修订新版（九年级上册，8 单元）
# 题型：S1 正向 / S2 逆向 / S3 语法要点 / S4 综合

## Unit 1 The Changing World
# S1 正向
"这几十年家乡变化"？###My hometown has changed a lot in the last 10 years.
# S2 逆向
"used to / have become" 区别？###used to(过去习惯) vs 现在完成(已变化)
# S3 语法要点
过去时 vs 现在完成时？###过去时表已发生；完成时表影响延续
# S4 综合
对比变化？̶###Now the roads are wide, and life has become easier.

## Unit 2 Inspiring People
# S1 正向
表达敬佩对象？###I admire the scientist who works for the country.
# S2 逆向
"It is kind of him..." 结构？###It is + 形容词 + of + 人 + to do
# S3 语法要点
It is... for/of sb to do？###for(事情) of(人的品质)
# S4 综合
介绍敬佩的人？I admire the teacher who helps us. It is kind of her to teach.

## Unit 3 Smart Learning
# S1 正向
宾语从句 that？###I think that practice makes perfect.
# S2 逆向
if/whether 宾语从句？###表示是否（不确定）
# S3 语法要点
that/if/whether 引导宾语从句？###陈述 that；一般疑问 if/whether
# S4 综合
谈论学习方法？I believe (that) we should review every day. I don't know if it works for everyone.

## Unit 4 Our Memory
# S1 正向
用疑问词宾语从句问记忆？###Do you know what helps our memory?
# S2 逆向
"wonder why" 用法？✓不知为何
# S3 语法要点
疑问词宾语从句语序？###主+谓语+疑问词+陈述语序
# S4 综合
谈记忆改善方法？###I wonder why we forget. Repeating after class helps us remember.

## Unit 5 Power of Ideas
# S1 正向
发明是被动被发明？###The telephone was invented in 1876.
# S2 逆向
"was invented" 是；被动语态的一般过去###被动语态（一般过去）
# S3 语法要点
一般现在/过去被动？###am/is/are + 过去分词；was/were + 过去分词
# S4 综合
谈发明与用途？###The mobile phone was invented in 1973．It is used for communication.

## Unit 6 Beyond Earth
# S1 正向
未来被动句？###Robots will be sent to Mars. / A station will be built soon.
# S2 逆向
"will be followed" ？###一般将来被动
# S3 语法要点
一般将来被动？###will be + 过去分词
# S4 综合
谈太空计划？A new telescope will be launched. Many secrets of space will be explored.

## Unit 7 Feel the Rhythm
# S1 正向
用关系从句描述喜欢的音乐？I like songs which have stars.
# S2 逆向
"that/which" 修饰？###指物/事情（关系代词）
# S3 语法要点
关系从句 that/which/who？###物用 which/that；人用 who
# S4 综合
介绍喜欢的音乐### I enjoy the music which is relaxing. It helps me calm down.

## Unit 8 More than a Game
# S1 正向
谈体育赛事激励？The players who play well inspire us.
# S2 逆向
主语一致用法？The team is strong.
# S3 语法要点
主谓一致？###单数主语+单数谓语对比
# S4 综合
谈体育精神？Sport is more than a game. It teaches us to keep going and work as a team.
