# 初中七年级上册 英语 背诵内容测试题
# 教材：人教PEP版 2024 修订新版（Starter 1-3 + Unit 1-7）
# 格式：问题###答案（# 开头为注释）

# ===== Starter Unit 1 Welcome! =====
# 核心句型：问候与自我介绍
早上好的英文问候？###Good morning.
"你好/再见/你好吗"基础问候？###Hello/Hi. How are you? — I'm fine, thank you.
询问"你叫什么名字"并回答？###What's your name? — My name is Peter. / I'm Peter.
初次见面"很高兴认识你"？###Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you too.
询问"你多大了"？###How old are you? — I'm 12 years old.

# ===== Starter Unit 2 Keep Tidy! =====
# 物品与颜色
询问"书包里有什么"？###What do you have in your schoolbag? — I have some books.
询问"帽子是什么颜色"？###What colour is the cap? — It's red.
描述物品位置？###Some books are in / on / under the box.
询问"这是你的尺子吗"？###Is this your ruler? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

# ===== Starter Unit 3 My Yard =====
# 事物与数量
指着东西问"这是什么/那是什么"？###What's this? — It's a rabbit. / What's that? — It's a duck.
问复数"这些/那些是什么"？###What are these/those? — They're rabbits.
问"它们有多少只"？###How many rabbits do they have? — They have three.

# ===== Unit 1 You and Me =====
# be动词：姓名/国籍/班级
用 be 动词介绍自己和他人？###I'm Peter. He's my friend. We're in the same class.
问"你在哪个班"？###What class are you in? — I'm in Class 1.
问"谁是你们的班主任"？###Who's your class teacher? — Ms Gao.
"where from"问国籍/来源？###He's from the US. / Where is he from? — He's from China.

# ===== Unit 2 We're Family! =====
# 家庭介绍 + 所有格
介绍家庭照片？###This is my family photo. / These are my parents.
问"他长什么样"并回答？###What does he look like? — He's tall and thin.
用名词所有格表示"我的"？###This is Tom's father. / It's Lily's book.
表达家庭成员间关系？###He is my father. She is my mother. They are my parents.

# ===== Unit 3 My School =====
# 学校 + There be + 方位
用 There be 表示"有……"？###There is a library in our school. / There are 40 students in my class.
问"图书馆在哪里"？###Where is the library? — It's next to the classroom.
方位介词表达位置？###The playground is in front of / behind / between / next to the building.

# ===== Unit 4 My Favourite Subject =====
# 学科 + 连词
问"你最喜欢的科目"？###What's your favourite subject? — It's English / math.
问"为什么"并用 because 回答？###Why do you like English? — Because it's interesting.
用 and/but/because 连接原因？###I like math and science, but I don't like history because it's hard.

# ===== Unit 5 Fun Clubs =====
# 能力 + can
问"你会什么"？###Can you play the guitar / swim? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
想加入俱乐部怎么表达？###I'd like to join the music / English club.
用 can 表达能力？###I can sing and dance. She can play the piano.

# ===== Unit 6 A Day in the Life =====
# 时间 + 日常作息
问"几点起床"并回答？###What time do you get up? — I get up at seven.
问日常作息（when/what time）？###When do you go to school? — At eight. / What time do you go to bed?
时间介词 at / on / in 的用法？###at seven o'clock（时刻）；on Monday（星期）；in the morning（时段）

# ===== Unit 7 Happy Birthday! =====
# 生日 + 序数词
问"你生日是什么时候"？###When is your birthday? — It's on June 1st.
用序数词表达日期？###first, second, third，如 May 1st, October 2nd, December 25th.
"你多大了"与生日？###How old are you? — I'm 13. My birthday is in May.
庆祝生日的活动表达？###We have a birthday party. Let's sing the birthday song.
