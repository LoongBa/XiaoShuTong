# 初中九年级 英语 背诵篇目清单

> 来源：教材原文复核（2026-09，对照《义务教育教科书·英语九年级上册》2024 修订新版）
> 基准版本：人教 PEP 2024 修订新版（基于2022课标）
> 说明：旧版 Go For It 九年级，2024 新版为全新主题式单元（8 单元），重写

## 一、九年级上册教材结构（8 主题单元）

| Unit | 主题 | Big Question | 核心句型 | 语法焦点 |
|------|------|-------------|----------|---------|
| 1 | The Changing World | What brings about changes? | What changes have taken place? / My hometown has changed. | 过去时 vs 现在完成时 |
| 2 | Inspiring People | Who inspires us? | Who do you admire? / It is kind of him to help. | It is...(of/for) to do |
| 3 | Smart Learning | What makes a good learner? | I think that... / I don't know if... | 宾语从句(that/if/whether) |
| 4 | Our Memory | How can we have a good memory? | Do you know what helps memory? | 宾语从句(wh-疑问词) |
| 5 | Power of Ideas | What do creative ideas bring? | The telephone was invented in 1876. | 被动(现在/过去) |
| 6 | Beyond Earth | Why do we explore space? | Robots will be sent to Mars. | 被动(一般将来 will be) |
| 7 | Feel the Rhythm | Why do we need music? | I like music which is relaxing. | 关系从句(that/which/who) |
| 8 | More than a Game | How does sport inspire us? | The players who play well inspire us. | 主谓一致 |
