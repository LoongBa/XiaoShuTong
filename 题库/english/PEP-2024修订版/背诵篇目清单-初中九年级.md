## 一、教材结构

### 九年级全一册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 How can we become good learners? | 学习方法 | study for a test, work with a group, ask...for | How can I...? |
| Unit 2 I think that mooncakes are delicious! | 节日文化 | lantern, dragon boat, mooncake, Christmas | I think that... I wonder if/whether... |
| Unit 3 Could you please tell me where the restrooms are? | 间接引语 | restroom, staff, grape, central, nearby | Could you tell me where/how/what...? |
| Unit 4 I used to be afraid of the dark. | 过去现在对比 | used to, silent, helpful, public, private | I used to... I didn't use to... |
| Unit 5 What are the shirts made of? | 被动语态 | made of/from/in, produce, process, product | What is/are... made of/from/in? |
| Unit 6 When was it invented? | 被动语态过去时 | invent, telephone, wheel, electricity, camera | When was it invented? Who was it invented by? |
| Unit 7 Teenagers should be allowed to choose their own clothes. | 情态动词+被动 | allow, drive, safety, earring, concentrate | Teenagers should be allowed to... |
| Unit 8 It must belong to Carla. | 推测 | must, might, could, can't, belong to | It must/might/can't be... |
| Unit 9 I like music that I can dance to. | 定语从句 | that, who, prefer, lyrics, Australian | I like music that/who... |
| Unit 10 You're supposed to shake hands. | 文化习俗 | bow, kiss, greet, value, drop by | You're supposed to... |
| Unit 11 Sad movies make me cry. | 使役动词 | make sb. do/make sb. adj., leave, pull | ...make(s) sb. (feel)... |
| Unit 12 Life is full of the unexpected. | 叙事 | unexpected, by the time, go off, wake up | By the time I..., I had... |
| Unit 13 We're trying to save the earth! | 环境保护 | litter, advantage, cost, recycle, reuse | We're trying to... |
| Unit 14 I remember meeting all of you in Grade 7. | 回忆展望 | remember doing, look forward to, meet the standard | I remember... I'm looking forward to... |
