# 初中八年级下册 英语 背景钩子（知识卡片）

> 用途：背诵时展示的记忆增强卡片，核心句型锚定 + 易混点对比 + 记忆钩子
> 版本：2026-09 对照 2024 修订新版（8 主题单元）

---

## Unit 1 Time to Relax（放松时间）
- **核心句型**：How do you spend your free time? / I exercise to stay healthy.
- **关键词**：hobby, free time, relax, read, positive
- **记忆点**：不定式作目的状语
- **易混点**：to relax（目的）vs to be relaxed（状态）
- **记忆钩子**："I draw to relax"—爱好 + 不定式目的

## Unit 2 Stay Healthy（保持健康）
- **核心句型**：You should... / What's wrong? I have a headache.
- **关键词**：healthy, exercise, rest, medicine, fever
- **记忆点**：should 建议 + 反身代词
- **易混点**：should（建议）vs must（必须）；myself vs yourself
- **记忆钩子**："You should see a doctor."—健康建议

## Unit 3 Growing Up（成长）
- **核心句型**：What makes you upset? / Although it's hard...
- **关键词**：upset, calm, positive, team, problem
- **记忆点**：连接词 although/until/so that
- **易混点**：although（虽然）vs until（直到）
- **记忆钩子**："Although it's hard, I keep going."—成长韧性

## Unit 4 The Wonders of Nature（自然奇观）
- **核心句型**：Mount Qomolangma is the highest mountain.
- **关键词**：nature, highest, wonder, explore, metre
- **记忆点**：形容词最高级 + 大数
- **易混点**：the highest（最高）vs more than（超过）
- **记忆钩子**："the+最高级" 描述自然之最

## Unit 5 Nature's Temper（自然之怒）
- **核心句型**：What were you doing when the typhoon came?
- **关键词**：typhoon, earthquake, disaster, storm
- **记忆点**：过去进行时
- **易混点**：过去进行（当时的进行）vs 一般过去（已发生）
- **记忆钩子**："were you doing when"—灾难时刻

## Unit 6 A Good Read（好书）
- **核心句型**：Have you ever read...? / I've already read...
- **关键词**：read, novel, book, secret, garden
- **记忆点**：现在完成时 + already/yet
- **易混点**：already（肯定/已）vs yet（否定/句末）
- **记忆钩子**："Have you ever read it?"—阅读经历完成时

## Unit 7 Crossing Cultures（跨文化）
- **核心句型**：How different are our cultures? / I have known him since 2010.
- **关键词**：culture, custom, manners, respect
- **记忆点**：现在完成时 + since/for
- **易混点**：since（时间点）vs for（时间段）
- **记忆钩子**："since 2010 / for 10 years"—跨文化问候

## Unit 8 Making a Difference（做出改变）
- **核心句型**：What can we do to help? / I have volunteered...
- **关键词**：volunteer, help, shelter, difference
- **记忆点**：现在完成时表达志愿经历
- **易混点**：help to do（搭配）vs make a difference（改变）
- **记忆钩子**："have volunteered"—志愿时长完成时
