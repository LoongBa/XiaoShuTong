# ===== Unit 1 Animal Friends =====
## Unit 1 Animal Friends（动物朋友）
- **核心句型**：What's your favourite animal? / Why do you like...? — Because... / Where are...from?
- **关键词**：fox, tiger, giraffe, penguin, wolf, clever, cute, scary, dangerous, in danger, save
- **记忆点**：动物名词复数 + 形容词描述
- **易混点**：Why（问原因）→ Because 回答；Where from（问产地）；What like（问外形/喜好）
- **记忆钩子**："Why do you like pandas?" → "Because they're cute!"（动物喜爱问答万能句）

# ===== Unit 2 No Rules, No Order =====
## Unit 2 No Rules, No Order（没有规则就没有秩序）
- **核心句型**：We must/mustn't... / We can/can't... / We have to... / Don't...
- **关键词**：rule, order, follow, late for, be quiet, keep clean, turn off, be on time
- **记忆点**：情态动词表达规则的肯定/否定
- **易混点**：mustn't（禁止）vs don't have to（不必）；can't（不允许）vs have to（不得不）
- **记忆钩子**："Rules keep us safe"——祈使句规则 + 情态动词规则双用法

---

# ===== Unit 3 Keep Fit =====
## Unit 3 Keep Fit（保持健康）
- **核心句型**：Is it yours? / How often do you play?
- **关键词**：keep fit, play sport, exercise, healthy, racket
- **记忆点**：名词性物主代词 + 频率副词
- **易混点**：物主代词 mine（n.）vs 形容词性 my（修饰名词）；频率 always > usually > often > sometimes > seldom > never
- **记忆钩子**："Is it yours?" "No, it isn't mine."（物品归属 → 物主代词表）

# ===== Unit 4 Eat Well =====
## Unit 4 Eat Well（吃得好）
- **核心句型**：What would you like to eat? Would you like A or B? I'd like...
- **关键词**：order, dish, soup, vegetables, salad, noodles, dumplings, rice
- **记忆点**：点餐表达 + 可数/不可数名词
- **易混点**：would like（想要）vs like（喜欢）；可数（apple）vs 不可数（rice/water）
- **记忆钩子**："Would you like rice or noodles?"——选择疑问句点餐

---

# ===== Unit 5 Here and Now =====
## Unit 5 Here and Now（此时此刻）
- **核心句型**：What are you doing? / I'm doing... / This is...speaking.
- **关键词**：now, at the moment, call, phone message
- **记忆点**：现在进行时 + 电话交际
- **易混点**：进行时（正在做）vs 一般现在（habit）；say/tell（说）vs speak（打电话说）
- **记忆钩子**："This is ... speaking. Could I speak to ...?"（电话进行时场景）

# ===== Unit 6 Rain or Shine =====
## Unit 6 Rain or Shine（晴雨无阻）
- **核心句型**：What's the weather like? It's raining/sunny...
- **关键词**：weather, sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy, snowy, warm, cold, temperature
- **记忆点**：天气问答 + 进行时连用
- **易混点**：weather（天气）vs whether（是否）；rainy（adj）vs rain（v/n）
- **记忆钩子**："Rain or shine"——天气影响活动与情绪（主题双问题）

# ===== Unit 7 A Day to Remember =====
## Unit 7 A Day to Remember（难忘的一天）
- **核心句型**：How was your...? What did you see/do? We went to...
- **关键词**：trip, museum, factory, theatre, farm, unforgettable
- **记忆点**：一般过去时（was/were + did）
- **易混点**：went（go 过去式）vs gone；-ed 三种发音 /t//d//ɪd/
- **记忆钩子**："How was your trip?" "It was wonderful!"（体验问答）难忘一天的时间线

# ===== Unit 8 Once upon a Time =====
## Unit 8 Once upon a Time（很久很久以前）
- **核心句型**：Once upon a time...  did you like the story?
- **关键词**：story, characters, ending, emperor, truth
- **记忆任务**：叙述故事的一段过去时
- **易混点**：inside 时间点（once upon a time 开始故事）vs；check out；说真话 tell the truth
- **记忆钩子**："King's New Clothes"（讲故事 + 过去时 + 诚实寓意）
