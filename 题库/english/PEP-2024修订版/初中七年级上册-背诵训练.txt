# 初中七年级上册 英语 背诵训练题库（分阶检索法）
# 教材：人教PEP 2024 修订新版（Starter 1-3 + Unit 1-7）
# 题型：S1 正向（中文→英文）/ S2 逆向（英文→中文·用途）/ S3 语法要点 / S4 综合
# 格式：题目###答案（# 开头为注释）

# ===== Starter Unit 1 Welcome! =====
## Starter Unit 1 Welcome!
# S1 正向检索
"早上好"用英语怎么说？###Good morning.
询问"你叫什么名字"？###What's your name? — My name is Peter.
# S2 逆向检索
"How are you?" 怎么回答？###I'm fine, thank you. / I'm good.
"Nice to meet you." 用于什么场合？###初次见面打招呼
# S3 要点默写
默写 be 动词三变位？###I am; He/She/It is; You/We/They are
# S4 综合默写
默写完整自我介绍？###Hello! My name is Peter. I'm 12 years old. I'm from China. Nice to meet you!

# ===== Starter Unit 2 Keep Tidy! =====
## Starter Unit 2 Keep Tidy!
# S1 正向检索
询问"书包里有什么"？###What do you have in your schoolbag?
询问"帽子是什么颜色"？###What colour is the cap? — It's red.
# S2 逆向检索
"What colour ...?" 用于？###询问颜色
"in / on / under the box" 的区别？###在盒子里 / 上 / 下
# S3 要点默写
默写询问颜色句型？###What colour is + 单数? — It's + 颜色. / What colour are + 复数?
# S4 综合默写
默写描述随身物品及位置？###I have a pencil box. My ruler is in it. The books are on the desk.

# ===== Starter Unit 3 My Yard =====
## Starter Unit 3 My Yard
# S1 正向检索
指着近处问"这是什么"？###What's this? — It's a rabbit.
询问复数"这些是什么"？###What are these? — They're ducks.
# S2 逆向检索
"What are these/those?" 回答用什么主语？###They（复数）
"this/that" 与 "these/those" 的区别？###近/远 + 单/复数
# S3 要点默写
how many 后接什么？###how many + 可数名词复数
# S4 综合默写
描述院子里的动物？###There are three rabbits and two ducks in the yard.

# ===== Unit 1 You and Me =====
## Unit 1 You and Me
# S1 正向检索
"你多大了"？###How old are you?
用 be 动词介绍新朋友？###This is my friend Peter. He's from the US.
# S2 逆向检索
"What class are you in?" 回答？###I'm in Class 1.
"Who's" 的完整形式？###Who is
# S3 要点默写

# ===== Unit 2 We're Family! =====
## Unit 2 We're Family!
# S1 正向检索
介绍父母？###These are my parents.
问"他长什么样"？###What does he look like? — He's tall and thin.
# S2 逆向检索
名词所有格 's 表示什么？###表示所属关系（Tom's father = 汤姆的父亲）
# S3 要点默写
默写询问外形容的句型？###What does he/she look like? — He/She is tall/short/thin...
# S4 综合默写
默写介绍家庭？###This is my family photo. My father is tall. My mother is kind. I love my family.

# ===== Unit 3 My School =====
## Unit 3 My School
# S1 正向检索
用 There be 表达"学校有图书馆"？###There is a library in our school.
问"图书馆在哪里"？###Where is the library? — It's next to the office.
# S2 逆向检索
"in front of / behind / between"？###在...前面 / 后面 / 之间
# S3 要点默写
there is 与 there are 的区别？###there is + 单数；there are + 复数
# S4 综合默写
描述学校布局？###There are many trees around our school. The library is between the gym and the classroom.

# ===== Unit 4 My Favourite Subject =====
## Unit 4 My Favourite Subject
# S1 正向检索
问"你最喜欢的科目"？###What's your favourite subject? — It's English.
问"为什么喜欢"？###Why do you like English? — Because it's interesting.
# S2 逆向检索
"because" 引导什么从句？###原因状语从句
# S3 要点默写

# S4 综合默写

# ===== Unit 5 Fun Clubs =====
## Unit 5 Fun Clubs
# S1 正向检索
"你会弹吉他吗"？###Can you play the guitar? — Yes, I can.
"我想加入音乐俱乐部"？###I'd like to join the music club.
# S2 逆向检索
"can" 情态动词表达什么？###能力或准许

# S4 综合默写

# ===== Unit 6 A Day in the Life =====
## Unit 6 A Day in the Life
# S1 正向检索
"你几点起床"？###What time do you get up? — I get up at seven.
"你几点去上学"？###When do you go to school? — At eight.
# S2 逆向检索
