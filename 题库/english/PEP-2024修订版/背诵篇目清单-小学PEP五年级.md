## 一、教材结构

### 五年级上册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 What's he like? | 人物描述 | old, young, funny, kind, strict, polite | What's he/she like? |
| Unit 2 My week | 星期与课程 | Monday~Sunday, wash clothes, watch TV | What do you have on Mondays? |
| Unit 3 What would you like? | 食物点餐 | sandwich, salad, hamburger, tea, fresh | What would you like to eat/drink? |
| Unit 4 What can you do? | 能力 | sing, dance, draw, cook, swim, play | What can you do? |
| Unit 5 There is a big bed | 房间描述 | clock, plant, bottle, bike, photo | There is a/an... / There are... |
| Unit 6 In a nature park | 自然公园 | forest, river, lake, mountain, hill | Is there a...? Are there any...? |

### 五年级下册

| 单元 | 核心主题 | 重点词汇 | 重点句型 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Unit 1 My day | 日常活动 | eat breakfast, have...class, play sports | When do you...? |
| Unit 2 My favourite season | 季节 | spring, summer, autumn, winter | Which season do you like best? |
| Unit 3 My school calendar | 月份节日 | January~December, New Year, Christmas | When is...? |
| Unit 4 When is Easter? | 序数词 | first~twelfth, twentieth | When is your birthday? |
| Unit 5 Whose dog is it? | 所有格 | his, hers, mine, yours, theirs | Whose... is this/that? |
| Unit 6 Work quietly! | 行为规范 | doing morning exercises, having...class | What are they/you doing? |
